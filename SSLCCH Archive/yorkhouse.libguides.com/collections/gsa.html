Gender & Sexuality Alliance

The York House School Gender & Sexuality Alliance (GSA) is a safe place to meet, make friends, and talk about issues and feelings. Our GSA aims to improve the school climate for everyone by promoting discussion about LGBTQ2SIA+ issues, and by encouraging greater understanding among students, staff, and parents alike. Our meetings take the form of discussions, book sharing, guest speakers, films, and more, and are open to adults and students in the senior school. The GSA meets 1-2 times a month. Look for e-mails and announcements about upcoming dates and meeting locations! 

Check out the full pamphlet for more details, and book reviews!
